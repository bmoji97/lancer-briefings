## Extract
The Huygens Free State is a founding member of the Free Huygens Coalition, a loose coalition of governments, corporations, merchant organizations, organized militias, and breakaway colonial authorities that jointly declared independence from Union during the Huygens Incident. Centered on the Huygens System's first colony: the temperate world of Vor Aris, the Free State provides much of the Coalition's political leadership, industrial output, and strategic direction.

While the Coalition publicly presents itself as an alliance of equals, intelligence suggests the Free State - with Jhamal Ross at its head - is its leader in all but name. Vor Aris manufactures the majority of the Collective's industrial components, coordinates much of its interstellar logistics, and hosts the political institutions responsible for maintaining cohesion among its otherwise disparate members. The loss of the Free State would not necessarily end the rebellion, but it would cripple the Collective's ability to wage coordinated war.

The Free State did not emerge spontaneously from the chaos of the Huygens Incident. Investigations conducted by Union Intelligence indicate that key elements within Vor Aris had spent years quietly preparing for secession. Resource stockpiles, military hardware, independent communications infrastructure, and sympathetic officials throughout the Huygens System appear to have been positioned well before the first declaration of independence. What appeared to the public as a sudden uprising now seems increasingly likely to have been a carefully orchestrated coup d'état.

## Government
The Free State's government is comprised of two chambers: the executive and legislative. However, in practice it operates much as the ancient Cradle Roman Empire, with the executive office holding vastly more power than the legislative. The Head of State is Chancellor Jhamal Ross, former Union Administrator assigned to Huygens, who is believed at this time to be the primary orchestrator behind the Huygens Incident. His motives are unclear at this point, though a psychological evaluation previous to his defection notes he is a gifted administrator, charismatic public speaker, and effective leader. System-wide, Ross has become the symbolic face of Huygens independence, much to the chagrin of the other factions within the Free Huygens Coalition.

Ross' cabinet below him consists of Ministers in charge of the various facets of government: industry, security, agriculture, etc. Each minister wields vast political power and unchecked control over their department. The legislative chamber, the First Congress, is largely at the whim of these ministers, who unabashedly bribe and blackmail the congressmen. Nominally a democratic state, elections to the federal government have been suspended for twenty years pending an anticipated Union response.
