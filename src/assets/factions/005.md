## Extract
Originally a biological research institute founded to research Atropos' native lifeforms. In the centuries since, the Caer Ovar Institute has become a renowned contributer in the field of necrorobotics. Using the recovered corpses of Atropos' various lifeorms, the Institute has manufactured deep space industrial equipment, sensitive lab equipment, and mechanised chassis.

Atropos was initially settled as a monastary world by an order of Franciscan-Buddhist monks and their followers. As the interest in the unique insectoid life native to Atropos increased, there were an increasing number of requests by outside groups to create a research outpost. Eventually, the settlers agreed that a small research outpost could be established on the northern edge of the Titan's Spine mountains under the supervision of the monks and planetary authorities. Researchers were permitted to study the native wildlife under strict restrictions. The discovery of late post-mortem neural activity in native specimens during autopsy eventually led to the Institute's dedication to necrorobotics. 

According to recovered intel, it seems largely removed from the day-to-day politics of the Coalition. Apart from major issues that would affect it or the system as a whole, the Institute retains its attention inward. It has accepted contracts with other Coalition members for its necrorobotics and technical expertise in exchange for raw materials, test pilots, and field testing opportunities. This suggests that its membership in the Coalition is purely transactional, and they are indifferent to Huygens' independence. 

## Administration
Adhering to a strict hierarchy, the Caer Ovar Institute is led by its Director, Dr. Kenson. The name does not match any preexisting records, so Kenson's identity is unknown at this time. Recovered communications paint a picture of a stoic and rational director who is far removed from the personnel below them. Unusual for the head of a scientific insitution, they are almost exclusively involved in the administration of the Institute, though they personally oversee select classified research projects. Otherwise they delegate the management of the multitude of projects to the various department heads and their project managers.

References have been made to the following departments:
- Xenobiology: Responsible for cataloguing and studying Atropos' native life
- Necrorobotics: Studies the preservation and potental of biological tissue after death
- Applied Sciences: Researches commercial applications 
- Mechanised Chassis R&D: Develops mechanised chassis for industrial and military purposes
- Manufacturing R&D: Develops manufacturing equipment used by the other departments
- Containment: Develops and maintains secure facilities for hazardous organisms, experimental technology, and testing
- Specimen Acquisition: Responsible for locating, recovering, and transporting requested specimens.

## Unverified Information
Independent reports pre-Incident indicate that the biological material used in Caer Ovar's machines does not exhibit properties conventional to preserved tissue. Specimens have reported to twitch without electrical stimulation, respond to external stimuli, and display activity after being disconnected from power supplies.

Recovered data mentions wildlife dubbed "titanforms" not previously reported pre-Incident. Particular mentions were made regarding a specimen designated "*TITAN-0*," described as "the oldest specimen exhibiting viable neural architecture currently in containment." Further mentions of "Titanform Vaults" were also recovered from corrupted data. 

Recovered communications imply that several senior researchers have worked directly under Dr. Kenson for considerably longer than records of Kenson have exsisted.
