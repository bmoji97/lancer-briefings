## Extract
The Oread II Mining Guild is one of the founding members of the Free Huygens Coalition and the dominant economic power within the Oread Orbital Sphere. Operating thousands of mining operations across Oread and its moons and the surrounding asteroid belts, the Guild controls an immense share of the Coalition's production of strategic minerals, fusion reactants, industrial alloys, and fabrication feedstock. Its foundries and refineries fuel much of the Coalition's war machine, making the Guild indispensable to the Coalition despite its often-contentious relationship with the its political leadership.

The Guild traces its origins to the Oread II Mineral Interest, a Harrison Armory chartered corporation that maintained a near-total monopoly over mining operations throughout Oread for nearly three centuries. While immensely profitable, the corporation became infamous for dangerous working conditions, crushing production quotas, debt slavery, and the routine employment of private security forces to suppress labor organization. Although repeated investigations by UAD regulators resulted in incremental reforms, many miners believed meaningful change would never come while the corporation retained control.

Thirty-four years before the Huygens Incident, a system-wide labor uprising culminated in the complete collapse of the Mineral Interest's corporate administration. Rather than nationalizing the company or transferring its assets to colonial authorities, the miners seized the mines, refineries, and orbital infrastructure themselves. In the years that followed, they reorganized the enterprise into the Oread II Mining Guild, a cooperative owned and governed by its workers.

Initially celebrated throughout Huygens as a triumph of worker solidarity, the Guild rapidly grew into one of the system's most powerful institutions. As production expanded and new leadership emerged, practical necessity gradually gave way to entrenched bureaucracy. Positions that had once rotated among elected representatives became permanent offices. Local councils surrendered authority to regional directors, who in turn deferred to an increasingly centralized executive body headquartered on Oread II.

## Administration
The Mining Guild is governed by the Grand Assembly, a body composed of representatives elected from individual mines, refineries, orbital stations, teamsters, and industrial cooperatives. In theory, every worker possesses a voice through this system, and every major decision originates with the Assembly.

In practice, meaningful authority rests with the Executive Directorate, whose members oversee production, logistics, finance, security, labor allocation, and external relations. Each director is elected from within the Grand Assembly, and with each position being elected for lifelong terms, are highly sought after. Although formally accountable to the Assembly, the Directorate controls the flow of information, resource distribution, and election administration throughout the Guild. Few proposals reach the Assembly without first receiving the Directorate's approval.

At its head is Guildmaster Mara Volkov, a former tunneling engineer who rose through the Guild's administrative ranks during its early years. Publicly, Volkov remains a passionate advocate for worker ownership and collective prosperity, often invoking the sacrifices made during the revolution against the Mineral Interest.

Internal reports describe an administration increasingly obsessed with production quotas, expansion, and industrial output. Director positions typically are acquired thorugh bribery, and rarely through campaigning. Mine supervisors are awarded bonuses almost exclusively on extraction targets. Workplace accidents are frequently concealed to avoid disrupting exports, and dissenting cooperative delegates often find themselves reassigned to remote frontier operations or removed from positions of influence through procedural means. While the language of solidarity remains ever-present, many of the Guild's workers quietly remark that their bosses simply exchanged tailored suits for work jackets.

## Unverified Information
Recovered data from Coalition ships refer to a classified initiative designated **Project COBALT LINE**, administered jointly by the Guild's Directorate and several senior officials within the Free Huygens Coalition. The nature of **Project COBALT LINE** remains unknown.

Recovered transmissions suggest extraordinary quantities of labor and strategic resources have been diverted into excavation efforts beneath Oread II's oldest mining complexes. Requests for specialized geological equipment, advanced reactor shielding, and high-security engineering personnel have increased dramatically over the past two years.
