## Extract
The Sierra Madre 21st Critical Assistance Mission (CAM-21) is the Department of Justice and Human Rights' current expeditionary intervention force assigned to the Sierra Madre Line. Critical Assistance Missions are designed to restore lawful governance following humanitarian catastrophes, civil collapse, natural disasters, and anti-Union insurgencies. Every component of CAM-21 exists to stabilize societies rather than conquer them, employing judges alongside marines, engineers alongside investigators, and diplomats alongside combat pilots.

Following the declaration of independence by hostile authorities and the widespread collapse of legitimate civil institutions throughout the Huygens system, the Third Committee designated the crisis as requiring a full-spectrum Critical Assistance Mission. As such, forces comprising former elements of other critical assistance missions were formed into a new CAM, the 21st in the Sierra Madre Line. CAM-21 has been granted overall operational authority over Union's intervention, with military assets—including the Union Navy's 32nd Fleet—serving in direct support of the Department's objectives.

Serving as the Mission's headquarters is the UNS-CV *Rest for the Wicked*, a heavily modified supercarrier unlike any vessel in the Navy's inventory. Although carrying aerospace wings and a substantial marine contingent, also cotnained within its armored hull are diplomatic suites, refugee processing centers, forensic laboratories, emergency hospitals, communications ministries, reconstruction planning offices, and command centers capable of coordinating the restoration of entire planetary governments. During deployment, the *Rest for the Wicked* becomes the administrative heart of Union authority in contested space.

## Command Structure
Unlike naval formations, the Sierra Madre 21st Critical Assistance Mission is organized around civilian authority. Ultimate operational control rests with the Mission Council, a multidisciplinary body composed of senior directors representing every major bureau participating in the intervention. Justice officials, humanitarian coordinators, reconstruction planners, logistics administrators, emergency medical directors, labor commissioners, and security representatives each possess equal standing during strategic deliberations.

Presiding over the Council is Director-General Helena Varga, one of the Department's most respected architects of post-conflict reconstruction. Having spent decades overseeing stabilization efforts across the Dawnline Shore, Varga is renowned for balancing uncompromising adherence to Union law with remarkable pragmatism in the field. Her philosophy emphasizes that military success without legitimate civil authority merely postpones future conflict.

Although the Mission directs the overall intervention, cooperation with partner organizations remains essential. Director-General Varga sits on the Intervention Fleet Task Force alongside Admiral Julus Morrone of the Union Navy's 32nd Fleet and Cassius Rourke of the Union Science Bureau's Lost Horizons Far Field Team. While each organization maintains operational independence within its own specialty, all strategic planning ultimately flows through the Mission Council, reflecting Third Committee's intent that Huygens be liberated through justice rather than occupation.

## Operational Capacity
CAM-21 is less an army than a self-contained civilization capable of deploying anywhere in Union space. Its personnel include judges, prosecutors, public defenders, investigators, mediators, labor inspectors, engineers, educators, economists, doctors, psychologists, social workers, emergency responders, infrastructure specialists, communications experts, and civil administrators, all protected by heavily trained DOJ/HR Peacekeeping Divisions and Union Auxiliary formations.

Its expeditionary assets include dozens of administrative transports, hospital ships, reconstruction tenders, refugee support vessels, rapid deployment carriers, mobile fabrication platforms, and orbital logistics stations capable of establishing functioning governments within weeks of liberation. Dedicated legal archives, forensic laboratories, and census bureaus accompany every deployment, ensuring that crimes committed during the conflict are documented while displaced populations receive legal recognition and access to Union services.

Despite its civilian character, the Mission is far from defenseless. Embedded Peacekeeping Divisions operate advanced mech formations trained specifically for urban security, hostage rescue, riot suppression, counterinsurgency, and the protection of civilian infrastructure. In total, the Sierra Madre 21st Critical Assistance Mission fields nearly six hundred thousand personnel, with over one hundred thousand permanently assigned aboard the *Rest for the Wicked*. Supported by hundreds of specialized spacecraft and reinforced by attached Union Auxiliary contingents, the Mission possesses the resources necessary to administer multiple planetary reconstruction efforts simultaneously. Its objective is not merely to end the Huygens conflict, but to ensure that, once the fighting ceases, a lawful and self-sustaining society remains in its place.
