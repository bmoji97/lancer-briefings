## Extract
A PMC that originated as a Mirrorsmoke Mercenary Company detachment. Split from MSMC in 4920u due to "operational disagreements" between the original unit commander and Mirrorsmoke executives. Ragnarok made a name for itself as  PMC that attaches itself to hopeless causes, only to turn around their clients' fortunes. As such, they have reaped the rewards from countless hostile takeovers and bloody uprisings. They have engendered loyalty from their soldiers by primarily recruiting from within client organizations.

Regardless of their tendency to take on impossible missions and engratiate themselves with their clients, they typically do not stay for long following the conflict's end. They prefer to move from place to place throughout the diaspora, therefore it is unusual that they have decided to throw their lot in with Coalition forces.

All Ragnarok pilots take their callsigns from ancient pre-fall Cradle mythologies. Originally, their namesakes came from Norse mythology, but upon taking on new soldiers from the various cultures they have met, this inspiration has since expanded to encompass the stories and mythos of a multitude of ancient civilizations. Typically, their namesakes are prominent figures in apocalyptic and end-of-days myths.

## Command Structure
Reflecting their origin as an MSMC unit, Ragnarok's command structure is typical for a PMC outfit. Their leader Atum is responsible for political/business negotiations and makes Ragnarok's tactical decisions himself. Originally a wing leader and one of the original members of Ragnarok following its split from MSMC, Atum still participates in combat operations on occassion. He leaves much of the logisitics to a dedicated administrative department, and each combat wing has its own handler who leads a team of administrative liasons and field mechanics.

Many of the pilots from the original Ragnarok unit were reported to still be with the company. Those who have fallen in battle or otherwise retired have had their callsigns and chassis taken up by successor pilots. At this point, it is unknown how many of the original pilots are still with Ragnarok, and their tradition of successors makes it difficult to positively verify any pilots' identity.

## Military Analysis
Six distict mechanised chassis wings have been verified so far. They are as follows: Wing A: Osiris, Wing B: Ahriman, Wing C: Fenrir, Wing D: Kalki, Wing E: Odin, Wing F: Set.

No ships from *Operation: Beachhead* have been positively identified as belonging to Ragnarok. Records pre-Huygens Incident only note their flagship *The Armageddon*, a supercarrier previously decommissioned by MSMC. 

## Unverified Information
Recovered intel has suggested that Ragnarok detachments have been lent to each of the member of the Free Huygens Coalition.
