## Extract
Previously House Matsunaga, a minor branch house under The House of Stone, the House of Gauntlets exiled from the Concern following a scandal involving its blatant oppression of its commoner labor force. The first Ungratefuls' uprising under House Ludra still being fresh in the House of Stone's memory, House Matsunaga was stripped of their titles, holdings, and economic contracts. Then and now, the House of Gauntlets maintains it was operating under orders from the Stonelords and was disposed of as a scapegoat.

In the century and a half since then House Matsunaga, retitled as the House of Gauntlets, has settled in the Huygens system operating first as a mercantile and security organization. As their influence grew, they became the defacto rulers of New Opis, eventually leaving their mercantile enterprises for administrative ones. By the time of the Huygens Incident, the house had transformed New Opis into a fortress world.

The House's decision to join the Free Huygens Coalition appears to be motivated by opportunity and less by animosity towards Union or an actual desire for independence. The Coalition provides the House of Gauntlets the territory and legitimacy to operate as they did pre-exile, as lords ruling over peasants. In exchange the House has offered up training from its most experienced military officers, administrators, and house company to fellow Coalition members.

## Government
The House of Gauntlets operates as a typical Baronic Household, with its people split between the Noble and Ignoble. The nobles belong to either the major house, House Matsunaga, or its constituent minor houses. Members of the major house are rarely related by blood, with talented individuals being adopted into the main house, and the embarassments and rejects quitely being shuffled off into the minor houses. This leads to an intense culture of cutthroat competition and bloody politicking.

At the top is the Lady of Gauntlets Shiori Matsunaga, who serves as the head of hte House, legal owner of its major assets, supreme commander of its military forces, and the final arbiter of legal disputes. Beneath her is a hierarchy of relatives, sworn retainers, corporate and government administrators, military officers, and her chosen vassals. She was voted into the position from among her peers in the major house, though her primary rivals were either shuffled off into dead-end appointments or died in convienent accidents.

New Opis itself is theoretically governed independently of the House with local administrators retaining nominal authority over civilian and economic affairs. But in practice, nearly every major institution - planetary security forces, industrial oversight boards, and transportation networks - ultimatelly answer to the House of Gauntlet's authority.

## Military Analysis
Capability and tactical analysis is unavailable at this time.

The Houses' flagship, the FKS *Rightful Vengeance* is a heavily modified Karrakin pattern Superdreadnought, the origins of which remain unclear. Containing modules from both Karrakin battleships and carriers, it is a one of a kind command carrier.

The only known quantity pre-Incident was the houses' House Company The Retakers and its elites: The Hound's Argument

## Unverified Information
Requested Karrakin records concerning the House of Gauntlets remain heavily redacted. Further declassified documents are unlikely to be released to us.

Hidden House of Gauntlets facilities are thought to be scattered throughout Huygens, their purpose or contents is unknown at this time.

Recovered data suggests that the House is related in some way to the pirate Rook of Rook's Raiders.
