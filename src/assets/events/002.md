Following their first combat deployment, Socialite Squadron celebrates with a massive bender, leaving Empress with no memory of the night and somehow earning the ire of her cousin, logistics officer Giordane LaFond III.

The squadron then settles into a stretch of routine patrols and salvage security. Aside from occasional fleeting sensor contacts, nothing happens, and the passage of time begins to blur together. Their quiet period ends when they are summoned to a debriefing with Commodore Justev Pezal, attended by Deck Chief Bubbles, Gladiator Squadron leader Shameer McTavish, Security Chief Illa Alarosa, and DOJHR liaison Corrine Drennan.

The debriefing becomes contentious. Bubbles and Highlander are publicly reprimanded for Gladiator Squadron's failure to prevent Ragnarok forces from boarding the William Shepherd, despite Gladiator having done so to protect Vanguard Squadron's retreat. Alarosa is questioned about the defensive weaknesses she identified before the battle, but when she attempts to challenge the fleet's planning herself, her concerns are dismissed. 7R1N17 openly objects, believing command is minimizing the lives lost during the battle. She causes enough of a disruption to be expelled and disciplined by the William Shepherd's Captain, though Corrine intervenes, ultimately shielding her from further punishment and privately telling her that she approved of the protest.

Outside of the briefing room, Bubbles sternly reprimands Highlander over her conduct during the debriefing, prompting Lunchpail to intervene on Highlander's behalf. Bubbles eventually relents, earning Highlander's gratitude. Meanwhile, Amos develops an increasingly ridiculous rivalry with Bubbles, repeatedly pestering him until their encounters devolve into mutual gun-pointing, usually ending with Bubbles firing a warning shot. Despite himself, Bubbles gradually warms to Amos' antics. During one interruption of a meeting between Bubbles and Alarosa, Alarosa promises Amos that she will pass along anything relevant she discovers about his missing family in Huygens.

At the same time, Cyris Ragan becomes intensely interested in Cornfed's recovered deflection module. His obsession with examining the technology leads him to haul components through much of the William Shepherd in a wheelbarrow before Cornfed, Cyris, and Amos finally settle down at the bar to discuss the research.

Socialite Squadron's reputation also begins to improve, particularly among the Union Navy Marines. Word spreads throughout the William Shepherd that they performed exceptionally during their first battle, going above and beyond expectations and attracting the Commodore's attention. They receive greater respect from the regular Union pilots with Cornfed getting invited to some poker games.
