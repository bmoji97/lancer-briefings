Lunchpail attempts to help Bigfoot deal with the guilt he feels over Stutter taking an attack meant for him and being grievously injured as as result. Unable to help him get over his anger, Lunchpail instead focuses it onto their enemy, the PMC Ragnarok. Bigfoot agrees to pass on any info he gets in exchange for some extra stims to help him focus for longer. As time passes Stutter becomes stable enough to be transferred to the UNS Mercy of Dawn for surgery and cybernetic replacements for his lost arm and leg. Lunchpail and Bigfoot visit him before the transfer, finding Limbo already there. Lunchpail and Limbo try to get Bigfoot to face his guilt again, but Bigfoot is overwhelmed and, unable to cope, leaves angrily. Deciding to let him cool off, and wanting to stay with Stutter, Lunchpail stays for coffee and card games with Limbo and later Brazil.

After his surgery, Stutter contacts Lunchpail over the Omninet and asks him to look after Vanguard Squadron until he can return. Meanwhile, Nikol “Rampage” Das, transferred from his nearly destroyed squadron into Vanguard, arrives aboard the William Shepherd. Lunchpail attempts to welcome him with jokes and light ribbing, but Rampage responds with hostility. Their confrontation escalates into a brief fistfight before Rampage finally calms down and begins warming to him. Rampage explains how his squad was wiped out by a different Ragnarok unit. That unit then went on to destroy his ship, and Rampage and the other surviving squadmate were only made it out because their squad leader, a veteran Union Regular, gave his life to save theirs.
