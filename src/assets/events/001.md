Socialite Squadron arrives back at the hangar bay first, mission accomplished. The deck crew is bored and restless, the sounds of battle have slowed and they know their work comes soon. The squad takes a second to decompress; some talking to familiar faces, some taking a moment for themselves, yet others attempt to leave. Commander Westman eventually herds them to their ready room, preparing their debrief.

Bubbles asks the hard questions, Nubby Nubs and 7R1N17Y check out, the others start to get angry. Behind them another squad makes it back, but they're in bad shape. Vanguard Squadron is down a member and one mech is badly damaged. Bubbles brings back their focus and gets through the debriefing. Dismissed, the group go back out to the hangar to find that Vanguard's leader Rigo 'Stutter' Hotek had to be cut out of his mech and is badly injured. They find the remaining members, Zachery 'Bigfoot' Weatherford-Swickheimer, Rafal 'Brazil' Kalua, and Pepita 'Limbo' Sprinkles in various states of shock and grief. They eventually get the full story out of them.

Vanguard had a similar mission to Socialite, but when they boarded the enemy ship, instead of finding nominal hostile forces, they found an unknown, hostile Lancer; callsign Fenrir. He made quick work of the squad, killing Klara 'Blackcomb' Aranov, and Vanguard made a hasty retreat. Chasing them all the way back to the William Shepherd, Stutter took fire for Bigfoot.

The members of Socialite Squadron take note of Fenrir, hearing the same name from their encounter with Ragnarok in the launch catapult. With nothing left to do in the Hangar Bay, they each go their own way for the day.
