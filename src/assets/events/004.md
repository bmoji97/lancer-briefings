Cyris eventually invites Cornfed aboard the USV Lost Horizons to see the prototypes resulting from his research. Cornfed brings Amos and Lunchpail, where they are greeted by Dr. Cassius Rourke, Cornfed's former squad leader from the Lost Horizons Far Field Team. Rourke has apparently awakened from cryosleep decades before Cornfed and now looks considerably older. He has since become head of the Far Field Team, and warmly welcomes Cornfed back. He also reveals that another former squadmate, Rusi Eblan, is awake and somewhere in the Huygens System—but apparently fighting on the opposite side of the conflict.

Cyris remains bizarrely distracted throughout the visit, repeatedly forgetting who Amos and Lunchpail are and confusing them with other people. Nevertheless, he demonstrates several prototypes:

- A handheld pilot version of the deflection module
- A mech-mounted grenade that can also create a bubble shield
- A more generalized but weaker version of the original deflection technology

Cornfed ultimately chooses the bubble-shield prototype for Socialite Squadron.

Back aboard the William Shepherd, Cornfed is summoned to Bubbles' office and presented with an opportunity for Socialite Squadron to undertake an elite mission, recommended by both Dr. Cassius Rourke and Corrine Drennan. Cornfed accepts immediately, trusting in Cassius' belief in him.

On the eve of their deployment, Empress is invited to a cocktail party by Giordane after nearly a month of silence. Finding the party strangely underwhelming, with barely enough people to justify renting out the entire lounge, Empress confronts her cousin over his anger with her. Finally relenting, he asks her to apologize for sharing an embarassing story of him in their youth. Laughing, Empress refuses, and instead convinces him to stop caring about such small things - telling him that people will only laugh at him for that if he lets it embarass him. Making up, she covertly invites more people to the party, saving some face for her cousin and arranging an appropriate send-off party for herself.

Socialite Squadron is subsequently deployed to infiltrate Null Harbor Drydock, the next target of the Union intervention fleet—marking the end of their period of relative quiet.
