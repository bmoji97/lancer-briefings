# Mission // #002

## The Battle for Null Harbor

*"Every gun that is made, every warship launched, every rocket fired signifies in the final sense, a theft from those who hunger and are not fed, those who are cold and are not clothed. This world in arms is not spending money alone. It is spending the sweat of its laborers, the genius of its scientists, the hopes of its children. This is not a way of life at all in any true sense. Under the clouds of war, it is humanity hanging on a cross of iron."*  


# Primary Objectives

- Secure the customs office corridor in Docking Arm 4
- Secure the Traffic Control Center
- Clear a path to Old Storage
- Investigate Warehouse 18C

# Secondary Objectives

- Recover Null Harbor census archive data
- Steal OPFOR supplies
- Investigate anomalous signals

# Stakes

- The people of Null Harbor
- Industrial capabilities of the Intervention Fleet
- Enemy presence in our quadrant of the Outer Plane
